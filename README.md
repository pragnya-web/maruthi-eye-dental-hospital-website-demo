🏥 Maruthi Eye & Dental Hospital Website

A modern, responsive healthcare website concept created for **Maruthi Eye & Dental Hospital, Gangavathi, Karnataka**.

The project focuses on creating a professional digital presence for a healthcare business with clear service information, contact options, and an online appointment-booking experience.

 📄 Website Sections
 🏠 Home
Introduces the hospital and provides quick access to appointment booking, contact information, and directions.
👁️ Eye Care
Includes information about eye-care services such as:

* Cataract Care
* Phacoemulsification
* Foldable IOL
* Retina Care
* Glaucoma Care
* OCT
* Humphrey Visual Field Analysis
* Green Laser
* YAG Laser

 🦷 Dental Care
A dedicated section for dental-care information and appointment requests.
🏥 Facilities
A visual section for presenting the hospital's facilities and diagnostic capabilities.

👨‍⚕️ Doctors
A structured section for displaying doctor profiles, specialities, qualifications, and consultation information.
 📅 Appointment Booking
The website provides an appointment-booking experience where users can select:

1. Department
2. Service
3. Doctor
4. Date
5. Time
6. Patient information
7. Confirmation

📍 Contact & Location
Provides hospital contact information, working hours, location and directions.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to design a professional online presence for a local healthcare business and demonstrate how a traditional hospital website can be improved through:

* Modern UI/UX
* Clear service presentation
* Responsive design
* Online appointment functionality
* Local business information
* Strong calls to action
